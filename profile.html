About

Jake Riddell is a designer and leader studying Global Business and Digital Arts with an Economics minor at the University of Waterloo. He currently serves as President of the GBDA Society, where he leads cross-functional teams and manages high-impact initiatives for over 500 peers.

Throughout his time at Waterloo, Jake has demonstrated a passion for the intersection of design and business. He's competed against 200 students to win the uXperience Design Jam twice, creating prototypes for Rogers and Sun Life that integrated cutting-edge technology with thoughtful user experience design. His leadership at the GBDA Society has resulted in social media campaigns reaching over 100,000 views and raising more than $5,000 in funding.

Jake is proficient in Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, HTML/CSS, and JavaScript. He specializes in user research, UX design, brand strategy, and project management. When he's not designing or leading student initiatives, he coaches wakeboarding, where he's trained thousands of athletes and built lasting relationships with a diverse clientele.
