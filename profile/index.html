About

Jake Riddell is a marketer, builder, and student of both business and design. He is pursuing a degree in Global Business and Digital Arts with a minor in Economics at the University of Waterloo, his education has taken him to study abroad at the University of Dundee. He is drawn to the space where brand and product meet, and to the work of turning a rough idea into something people actually use.

Jake is working as a Product Marketing Co-op at Socialpruf, where he owns social media campaigns, rebuilt and optimized the marketing site, and shaped growth strategy for a growing startup. Before that, he served as President of Waterloo's Global Business and Digital Arts Society, leading a student team and representing the program at pitches, events, and industry conversations. Across both roles he has learned to move comfortably between strategy and execution, and to take ownership when a project needs someone to carry it.

His hands-on experience spans UX design, brand strategy, web development, and social media, and he works fluently in Claude Code, Figma, the Adobe Creative Suite, Framer, WordPress, and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
